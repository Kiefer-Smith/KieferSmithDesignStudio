Fractured Illusions Revisited is a first-person horror game that is being recreated in Unreal Engine 5; it was initially made in Unreal Engine 4 for a college project. It is being developed in its original vision. The game is set in an abandoned hospital where you play as a patient who has just awakened after being experimented on by an unknown group. Taken from a fancy hotel during your relaxing holiday to this new horror-filled environment, you have no choice but to escape.

The main objective is to uncover notes and diary entries-anything that will provide information about your predicament. With no weapons at hand, you must keep your wits and your fancy flashlight nearby to help you escape while encountering jump scares . The main question is: Is everything you are experiencing even real?

Inspired by Silent Hill & Layers of Fear.
