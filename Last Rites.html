Last Rites

Level Designer • 1 Month • Unity • 3 Person Team • Meta VR • VR Horror Escape Room

Overview

This project was a 1-month development effort for Meta VR headsets. As the level designer, I focused on creating an immersive horror experience through detailed environment design, atmospheric tension, and carefully crafted gameplay mechanics that build tension and suspense. Small puzzles and exploration elements are integrated to enhance player engagement and encourage discovery within the eerie game world.

Responsibilities

  • Level layout
  • Navigation clarity & player flow
  • Puzzle and exploration design
  • Internal playtesting iteration

Design Challenge

The main challenge was creating an immersive horror experience within the constraints of a 1-month development timeline and a 3-person team. Balancing atmospheric tension with engaging gameplay mechanics, while ensuring clear navigation and player flow, required careful design iteration and attention to detail throughout the development process.

Design Approach

To address the design challenge, I focused on creating a cohesive and immersive horror experience through detailed level design, environmental storytelling, and carefully crafted gameplay mechanics. I iterated on level layouts to enhance navigation clarity and player flow, while integrating small puzzles and exploration elements to encourage discovery within the eerie game world. Regular playtesting sessions allowed me to gather feedback and make adjustments to improve the overall player experience.

Layout Iteration

Regular playtesting sessions allowed me to gather feedback on the level layout, pacing, and player experience. I made adjustments to environmental details and puzzle design based on this feedback to enhance immersion and improve the overall horror experience. Iteration focused on balancing tension with engaging gameplay mechanics while ensuring clear navigation and player flow throughout the game.

The game was originally a regular living room scene which I tuned into a horror environment by adjusting lighting, adding fog, and introducing environmental storytelling elements. Navigation improvements were made by guiding players using lighting cues and environmental details.

Iteration Process

Regular playtesting sessions allowed me to gather feedback on level layouts, pacing, and player experience. I made adjustments to jumpscare placement, environmental details, and puzzle design based on this feedback to enhance immersion and improve the overall horror experience. Iteration focused on balancing tension with engaging gameplay mechanics while ensuring clear navigation and player flow throughout the game.

Outcome

The final build delivered a small but immersive VR puzzle experience that encouraged exploration and environmental interaction. Through iteration and playtesting, puzzle readability and object placement were improved to ensure players could progress naturally through the space. The project helped deepen my understanding of designing interactions for VR, particularly how spatial layout and visual cues can guide player behaviour without relying on explicit instructions.
