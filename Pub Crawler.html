Pub Crawler

Level Designer • 2 weeks • Unity • 4 Person Team • Android • Classic 2D Sidescrolling Platformer

Overview

A fast-paced mobile action game developed over a two-week sprint. Designed to deliver clear objectives and structured progression.

Responsibilities

  • Level layout
  • Enemy placement & pacing balance
  • Navigation clarity & player flow
  • Made changes based on feedback

Design Challenge

The main challenge was delivering meaningful level progression within strict time constraints while maintaining readability on small mobile screens.

Design Approach

I structured levels around clear visual landmarks and controlled enemy density to manage pacing. Sightlines were carefully designed to reduce player confusion and support intuitive navigation.

Level Layout Iteration

Early layouts suffered from pacing spikes and unclear navigation. By restructuring enemy spacing and improving landmark visibility, player flow became more readable and consistent.

Iteration Process

Through playtesting, I adjusted hazard placement and made collectible rewards more visible to enhance player engagement and flow. Iteration focused on balancing challenge with readability for short play sessions. For the collectibles I made the pints animate side to side as a way to draw the player's eye and make them more enticing to collect. I also made sure to space out hazards in a way that created a consistent rhythm of challenge and reward as players progressed through the level.

Outcome

The final build delivered tighter encounter pacing and improved spatial readability. Structured iteration significantly enhanced player flow and overall engagement during short play sessions.
