Fractured Illusions College Project

Fractured Illusions began as a project for college. For this project, I decided to create a horror game. The main goal was to answer the question of what makes horror games so scary. I chose to make a game because I have always wanted to ever since I was young, and I felt that this was a perfect opportunity. Having grown up with horror games, I wanted to apply the skills I have learned in college and independently to develop such a project. Since this was a one-person endeavor, I managed all the roles required for it.

I covered roles such as Game Design, Level Design, Story Design, UI/UX Design, some 3D Asset Modeling, Project Mangement, Production, and Game Direction. For most of the level assets and sound assets, I utilised free resources that were available for use. Before submitting the game, I had several people test it to hopefully catch any bugs and ensure that the jumpscares had their intended effect on the player. The game was presented at a FIS showcase in May 2023, with some players hesitant to play it! Due to time constraints with the college deadline, the level wasn't as detailed as I had hoped to achieve.

Door & Button Interaction System

For this project, I wanted to add a feature that will require the player to solve a small puzzle to reach the end of the level. This involves pressing a switch to open a locked door. Some of the switches will be in a room that is locked, requiring the switch to be pressed to open the room's corresponding door. The pathway to the switches will require the player to take an alternate route due to the path being blocked by debris. To assist the player, red lights have been placed to guide them to the switches.

When a switch is pressed, a small cutscene will play, showing the door opening for the switch that was activated. With pathways being blocked, this offers the opportunity for the player to think about how to reach the switch they need to interact with. To access the switch, the player may need to crouch through openings in a wall or find a switch that opens a locked room to reach another switch and progress further. This also offers the opportunity to include jump scares and sounds to make the player feel uneasy giving the player the sensation they are being followed which would make the player not want to turn around.

Jumpscares System

In this project, I added two small jumpscares for the player to encounter. Apart from the unsettling atmosphere of the game, the player will see a wheelchair being pushed out of the room, followed by the door slamming shut . Following this, the player will encounter a woman running across the hallway into the next room, from which the door will also slam. These jumpscares were created using sequencers that are triggered by the player entering the area.

Sound System

The sound for this project aimed to be unsettling, evoking a sense of uneasiness and creating the feeling that something is about to happen. The main music during gameplay is by MYUU, accompanied by some free audio assets to enhance the scary atmosphere. During the final hallway leading to the switch, I used audio of whispering and a demon laughing to instill terror in the player.

Further Details

Fractured Illusions first started out as a game that would take about an hour or 2 to complete. Being set in a hospital with six floors with the player being on the sixth making their way to the first floor to escape. Over time the project was reviewed by my lecturers who in turn advised I scale the project down massively. Which I did, with the game ending up jut being a level with a time limit with the goal to escape.

For the project I used free Unreal assets and bought some packs to help fill the level doing this made it easier to focus on getting the game in a playable state from start to finish. With a particular focus on the level & game design. My design document that is on this page shows that I studied other games and what people in the industry says go into what makes a game a fun game.
