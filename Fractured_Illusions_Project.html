Fractured Illusions College Project

Fractured Illusions began as a project for college. For this project, I decided to create a horror game. The main goal was to answer the question of what makes horror games so scary. I chose to make a game because I have always wanted to ever since I was young, and I felt that this was a perfect opportunity. Having grown up with horror games, I wanted to apply the skills I have learned in college and independently to develop such a project. Since this was a one-person endeavor, I managed all the roles required for it.

I covered roles such as Game Design, Level Design, Story Design, UI/UX Design, some 3D Asset Modeling, Project Mangement, Production, and Game Direction. For most of the level assets and sound assets, I utilised free resources that were available for use. Before submitting the game, I had several people test it to hopefully catch any bugs and ensure that the jumpscares had their intended effect on the player. The game was presented at a FIS showcase in May 2023, with some players hesitant to play it! Due to time constraints with the college deadline, the level wasn't as detailed as I had hoped to achieve.

This page showcases the parts of the project I am most proud of. For deeper insight into this project, it is available on ArtStation.
