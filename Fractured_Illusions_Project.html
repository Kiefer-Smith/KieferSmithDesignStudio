About & Resume

Fractured Illusions first statrted off as a project for college for this project I decided to make this horror game. The main goal for this project was to answer the queston of what is it that makes horror games so scary? I chose to make a game as I have always wanted to and I felt that this was a perefect opportunity as I grew up with horror games I wanted to use my skills that I have learned in college and in my own time to create to develop such a project. As this was a one person project I did all the roles that were needed for such a project.

The main objective is to uncover notes and diary entries-anything that will provide information about your predicament. With no weapons at hand, you must keep your wits and your fancy flashlight nearby to help you escape while encountering jump scares . The main question is: Is everything you are experiencing even real?

Inspired by Silent Hill & Layers of Fear.
