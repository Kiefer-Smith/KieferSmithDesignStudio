About & Resume

Hello and welcome to my portfolio!

I'm a game designer with six years of extenisve knowledge in the use of the Unreal Engine from my time in college. In college I made two games in Unreal one which was a first person horror game and the other was a 2D one button sidescroller. As part of my time in college I had to undertake work experience I chose to work at Nebula Interactive. Here I got experience as a Junior Level Designer and Game Tester.

Currently I am working on a personal project in Unreal Engine 5, the project is Fractured Illusions that I made for college which is being remade in it's original vision.
